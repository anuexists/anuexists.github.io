Projects

  1. Verifying Recurrent Neural Networks using Invariant Inference

    Reading Project - CMI, India | Dr. M. Srivas (CMI), Dr. K. Madhukar (IITD)

    Sept 2023 - Dec 2023

    Summary: Deep neural networks are powerful but opaque, making it hard to ensure their correctness. The paper focuses on verifying recurrent neural networks, vital for applications like speech recognition.

    Details/Approach: The approach relies on inferring invariants to simplify the verification of recurrent networks. It transforms a complex problem into simpler, non-recurrent ones. The proposal includes proof-of-concept experiments demonstrating its superiority over existing methods, with orders-of-magnitude better performance.

  2. Classification of Grapevine pollen using 3D CNN

    Guided Project - CMI, India | Dr. S. Das(CMI), Dr. P. Misra(CMI), Dr. Doris Barboni(IFP)

    Aug 2023 - Dec 2023

    Summary: It is the continuation of the Internship project. Tried to solve the problem using 2D CNN models, but the results achieved were not good enough. Later tried to apply 3D CNN models to gain better accuracy in the classification during this part of the project.
